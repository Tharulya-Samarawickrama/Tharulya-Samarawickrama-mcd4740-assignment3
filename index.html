Our Collections

Functional Pottery, Handmade in Western Australia

Stoneware, porcelain, terra cotta and functional pottery. Made with non-toxic, food-safe Australian clays and glazes, every piece is crafted for everyday strength and durability.

  • Refined provincial ceramics—made by hand

    Every piece of Tidal Clay Co. pottery is handcrafted in West Cape Howe, Western Australia. Simple, useful, and beautiful, ceramic collections are inspired by the beauty of nature. Equal parts rugged and delicate, organic neutrals draw out the quiet elegance of country coastal living.

  • Provincial elegance

    Raw on the outside with satin glazed interiors, subtle colours evoke old world charm. Incorporating botanicals and other natural objects creates irresistibly textured items—strong enough for everyday use. Made with non-toxic, food-safe Australian clays and glazes, each piece is crafted for strength and durability.

  • Make moments memorable

    The distinctive style of Tidal Clay Co. means no two pieces are alike. They are made to celebrate everyday moments, from family meals and bunches of flowers to warming cups of tea.
